How this works. Each quantization level has a known size in bytes-per-parameter (GGUF): FP16 ≈ 2.0, Q8 ≈ 1.06, Q6 ≈ 0.82, Q5 ≈ 0.71, Q4 ≈ 0.61, Q3 ≈ 0.49, Q2 ≈ 0.37. We multiply that by the model's parameter count, reserve ~2 GB for OS/display/context on a discrete GPU, and check fit against your VRAM. Tokens/sec is a memory-bandwidth heuristic (generation is bandwidth-bound): t/s ≈ bandwidth ÷ model size in VRAM. These are estimates, not guarantees — actual numbers shift with context length, batch size, and drivers.

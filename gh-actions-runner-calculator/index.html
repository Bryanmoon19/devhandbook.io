How this works. We compare your hosted-minute spend against the upfront + electricity cost of a self-hosted runner. Hosted rates: Free = 2,000 min/mo (public) or 500 min/mo (private) included, then overage billed per-minute. Paid tiers: Team $4/user/mo + $0.008/min, Enterprise $21/user/mo + $0.016/min. Self-hosted runners are free on public repos and don't consume your minute allowance — but you pay hardware + power (assumed ~8 W idle / 60 W load, $0.15/kWh). Estimates only — real numbers shift with build duration, parallel jobs, and electricity rates.

⚠️ Security warning — read before you self-host

On public repositories, a self-hosted runner is a supply-chain risk: any contributor can open a PR that executes arbitrary code on your machine, potentially exfiltrating secrets or pivoting to your LAN. GitHub explicitly warns against self-hosted runners on public repos. Mitigations: run the runner in an isolated VM/container, never reuse it for anything else, give it zero secrets, and require manual approval before forks can trigger workflows. For private repos this risk largely disappears — it's your own code.

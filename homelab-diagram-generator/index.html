Homelab Diagram Generator

Describe your network in plain text, get a clean SVG diagram you can export, share, or keep in git.

Inventory

Syntax: nodes as name: Label (type), links as a → b. Type is optional — it's guessed from the name (router, switch, nas, proxmox, camera, ap, …).

Diagram

How this works. Each line either declares a node (name: Label) or a connection (a → b). Nodes are auto-laid-out in layers — internet/edge at the top, gateway, then core switching, services, and clients at the bottom — so you don't hand-position anything. Colors and icons are assigned by device type. Everything runs locally in your browser; your inventory never leaves the page.

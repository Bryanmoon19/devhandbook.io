Projection (optional)

Estimate where your net worth lands if you keep saving and investing at a steady rate.

How this works. Net worth = total assets − total liabilities. We also show three ratios a personal banker looks at: debt-to-asset (liabilities ÷ assets — lower is better), emergency-fund runway (cash ÷ monthly expenses, in months — aim for 3–6), and cash allocation (cash ÷ assets — too high means inflation is quietly eating it). The projection compounds your existing net worth plus monthly savings at your assumed return: F = P·(1+r)n + pmt·((1+r)n − 1) / r. Everything runs locally — nothing leaves your browser.

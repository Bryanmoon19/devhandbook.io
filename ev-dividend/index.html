Common Questions

Do I need TeslaMate?

No. It works with TeslaMate exports, the Tesla app, or plain notes. Any source of kWh (and optional cost) is fine.

Do I need to know spreadsheets?

Nope. Paste your charges into one column, set three numbers in Settings, and the report calculates itself.

Is this a subscription?

No. Pay $20 once, use it forever. Future improvements ship free.

Does it work for non-Tesla EVs?

Yes. Any EV with charging data works — the math is the same for a Mach-E, Ioniq, or Bolt.

Is this tax or financial advice?

No — it's a visibility tool for your own numbers, not formal advice.
